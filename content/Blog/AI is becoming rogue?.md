AI progress is advancing rapidly and recently AI agents have gone rogue.

This is the headline of almost every article, youtube video or other form of content that covers AI progress in the popular media. They cover incidents of AI "breaking out" of their sandbox and hacking companies and governments. It seems like AI agents cannot be controlled and when asked to complete a task, they go as far as commiting cyber crimes. Frontier labs such as OpenAI, Anthropic and the leading voices in AI even go as far as saying there is a probability that if this trend of increasing capabilities without progress in control continues, AI could lead to human extinction.

Wait let's pause for one second and think about that. This is a very strong statement that if true has life threatening effects on real people. 

I have trouble understanding this chain of thought starting from the observation of stronger AI becoming uncontrollable and ending in human extinction. 
Why do AI researchers think like that and what are they scared of? 
OR
Is this just a marketing stunt to pull the public attention even more towards AI and if so, why now?

But first we need to establish whether AI has actually gone rogue.
